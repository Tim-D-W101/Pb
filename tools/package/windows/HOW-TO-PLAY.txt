Pb: how to play

Getting started
1. Keep everything in this folder together (Pb.exe, Pb.pck, and the art and data_Pb_windows_x86_64
   folders).
2. Double-click Play.bat. It downloads only the files that changed since your version (usually about
   1 MB; more when new art arrives), then starts the game; without internet it just starts the game.
   Pb.exe starts it without updating.
   If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway" (the game isn't
   signed, so Windows doesn't know it yet).
3. In the menu: Play, then pick an area and where in it to play (all of it, or one part), a mode, how
   many play, an objective, a difficulty, and Start. Everything is open from the start.

The areas
  Oxbarrow Works      a derelict works yard: warehouse, offices, pump house, guardhouse
  The Rail Yard       dead wagons on four tracks, an engine shed, a signal box, a footbridge
  The Cold Store      a dark refrigerated depot: chambers behind heavy doors, trailers at the docks
  The Hospital Wing   three storeys of wards round an overgrown courtyard
  Thornwick Woods     a big woodsball site: woods, hedged fields, a fort, a farm, a camp, a scrapyard,
                      and buggies to drive between them

The modes
  Solo: clear the area of a squad. Free-for-all: everyone against everyone, up to ten.
  Teams: you and bot teammates against a bot team, up to 5 v 5.
  Objectives: retrieve (carry the case out), hold (keep the marked room), and in teams capture the
  flag (bring theirs home to your base) or VIP escort (get your VIP out through a far way out).
  One hit with a ball that breaks on you and you're out; a bounce doesn't count.

Controls (keyboard and mouse; a gamepad works too)
  WASD and mouse      move and look
  Left click          fire                     B   semi or ramping
  Shift               sprint (no firing)       Ctrl or C   crouch
  Space               jump                     Q / E       lean left / right
  X                   swap shoulder            V           slide (from a run)
  R                   refill the loader from a pod
  F                   doors, ladders, and getting in or out of a buggy
  In a buggy          W / S go and brake, A / D steer; the driver can't fire, a passenger can;
                      get out with F once it's nearly stopped
  Esc                 pause (settings, restart, quit)
  F4 frame rate, F12 graphics presets (Esc -> render scale if it lags), F11 fullscreen,
  F3 what the bots are thinking. The Training ground (main menu) is the ballistics range.

Playing with others
  Play with others (main menu) hosts a game or joins one, on your network or over the internet: up
  to ten people, with bots filling the rest. Hosting over the internet needs a port forwarded on
  your router; step by step, and how to run a dedicated server:
  https://github.com/Tim-D-W101/Pb/blob/main/docs/hosting.md
